# Hakeem

### One Medical Record. Smarter Healthcare.

---

## Description

**Hakeem** is an integrated healthcare platform that connects patients with doctors, hospitals, laboratories, and radiology centers in one seamless ecosystem. It aims to simplify the patient's healthcare journey by providing faster access to trusted medical services, advance appointment booking, and a unified medical record that securely organizes the patient's medical history, prescriptions, laboratory results, and radiology reports.

By connecting healthcare providers through a unified platform, **Hakeem** helps reduce waiting times, improve communication, and enable healthcare facilities to prepare for patients before their arrival—especially in urgent and critical cases. The platform is designed to make healthcare more connected, accessible, and efficient for both patients and healthcare providers.

## Project Overview

**Hakeem** is an integrated healthcare platform designed to simplify and connect the patient's healthcare journey in one place. The application connects **patients, doctors, hospitals, laboratories, and radiology centers**, allowing patients to access trusted healthcare providers faster and more efficiently.

The platform provides a **unified medical record** for each patient, containing their medical history, medical information, prescriptions, laboratory results, and radiology results. With the patient's permission, authorized healthcare providers can access the relevant medical information needed to provide better and faster care.

**Hakeem** also enables patients to search for suitable healthcare providers, book appointments in advance, and reach hospitals or medical centers without wasting unnecessary time.

In critical cases, the patient's medical information can be shared with the receiving hospital in advance, allowing the hospital and medical team to prepare for the patient's arrival and begin the appropriate care as quickly as possible.

The project will be presented as a complete **UI/UX case study**, covering the research, problem definition, user journeys, information architecture, user flows, wireframes, visual design, prototype, and usability testing.

## Project Objectives

- **Simplify the patient's healthcare journey** by connecting different healthcare services through one platform.
- **Create a unified medical record** that helps authorized healthcare providers understand the patient's medical history.
- **Reduce waiting and treatment delays** by enabling advance appointments and preparing healthcare providers before the patient's arrival.
- **Connect patients with trusted healthcare providers**, including doctors, hospitals, laboratories, and radiology centers.
- **Improve communication between healthcare providers** by making relevant patient information available when needed and with the patient's permission.
- **Help healthcare providers reach more patients** through an integrated digital platform.

---

## Project Scope

- **Patient App:** Search for doctors, hospitals, laboratories, and radiology centers and book healthcare services.
- **Unified Medical Record:** Store and organize the patient's medical history, prescriptions, laboratory results, and radiology results.
- **Doctor Services:** View authorized patient information, manage appointments, review medical history, and provide digital prescriptions or medical requests.
- **Hospital Services:** Receive patient information before arrival and prepare for scheduled or critical cases.
- **Laboratory & Radiology Services:** Receive bookings and upload laboratory or radiology results directly to the patient's medical record.
- **Healthcare Journey Tracking:** Connect different stages of treatment and make the patient's journey easier to follow.

> **Final Deliverable:** A complete UI/UX case study and high-fidelity prototype for the **Hakeem healthcare ecosystem**, demonstrating the end-to-end healthcare journey across patients, doctors, hospitals, laboratories, and radiology centers.

## Project Plan (5 Weeks)

### Week 1 — Research & Problem Definition

- Define the healthcare problem and project goals.
- Identify the main user groups.
- Conduct user research and surveys.
- Analyze existing healthcare platforms and competitors.
- Identify user pain points and opportunities.
- Define the problem statement and How Might We questions.

### Week 2 — UX Strategy & Information Architecture

- Create user personas for the main user groups.
- Develop empathy maps.
- Map the patient's current healthcare journey.
- Define the ideal future healthcare journey.
- Create the information architecture.
- Design user flows and task flows for the main features.

### Week 3 — Wireframing & UX Design

- Create low-fidelity wireframes.
- Design the main patient flows.
- Design doctor, hospital, laboratory, and radiology workflows.
- Design the unified medical record experience.
- Review and iterate based on feedback.

### Week 4 — UI Design & Prototyping

- Create the visual design direction.
- Develop the design system.
- Define typography, colors, components, and UI patterns.
- Create high-fidelity screens.
- Connect the screens into an interactive prototype.

### Week 5 — Testing, Iteration & Case Study

- Conduct usability testing.
- Identify usability issues.
- Improve the interface based on user feedback.
- Finalize the prototype.
- Document the design decisions and research findings.
- Prepare the final **Hakeem UI/UX case study** and presentation.
